### 2. NAP (Name, Address, Phone) Consistency
- Consistent business information across all pages
- Multiple NAP display variants (header, footer, contact)
- Proper formatting for local search engines
- Click-to-call and email functionality

### 3. Multi-language SEO Optimization
- **Hungarian version**: "Nagy Levi Marketing"
- **English version**: "Levi Nagy Marketing"
- Proper hreflang implementation
- Language-specific meta tags and structured data
- Localized content and keywords

### 4. Local SEO Meta Tags
- Geographic meta tags (geo.region, geo.placename, ICBM)
- Local business Open Graph properties
- Location-specific title tags and descriptions
- Canonical URLs with language variants

### 5. Google Business Profile Integration
- Ready-to-use Google Business Profile component
- Reviews display widget
- Direct link to Google Business listing
- Integration suggestions for optimal local SEO

## Google Business Profile Setup Recommendations

1. **Claim and verify** your Google Business Profile
2. **Complete all sections**: business hours, photos, services, description
3. **Regular updates**: Use Google Posts for announcements and offers
4. **Review management**: Respond to all reviews promptly and professionally
5. **Photo optimization**: Add high-quality photos of your office, team, and work
6. **Service area**: Define your service area (Budapest + 50km radius)
7. **Categories**: Select primary category "Marketing Agency" and relevant subcategories
